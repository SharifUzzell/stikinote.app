Privacy Policy

Effective 14 November 2026

Stiki is a notes app. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no servers of its own. We do not collect, store, sell, or share any information about you. This policy explains what the app does with your data on your device and in your iCloud.

Your notes

Everything you write — notes, checklists, photos, sketches, voice notes, stickers, reminders and Stacks — is stored on your device. If you are signed in to iCloud, Stiki syncs your notes between your devices through Apple's CloudKit, in a private database only your Apple Account can access. We cannot read it. Delete the app's data from iCloud at any time in Settings → Apple Account → iCloud → Manage Storage.

Widgets

Widgets show the notes you pin to them. They read the same data store on your device; nothing leaves it.

Notifications and alarms

Reminders you set are scheduled as local notifications on your device (or, for urgent reminders on iOS 26, as system alarms). Stiki does not send push notifications from any server.

Camera, microphone and photos

With your permission, the camera scans text and documents into a note, the microphone records voice notes, and the photo library supplies backgrounds and photos for notes. These are used only for the note you are editing and stay in your notes.

Purchases

Stiki Premium is sold through the App Store. Apple processes the payment; we receive no payment details. Purchases are tied to your Apple Account and can be restored on any of your devices.

Children

Stiki does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children, because it collects no data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published here with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about privacy: hello@stikinote.app.
