Sticky notes for your Home Screen.

Write a note, pin it as a widget. Build a Stack of quotes or affirmations and let it cycle through your day — on the Home Screen, the Lock Screen, and StandBy.

Join the beta on TestFlight What's inside
  • iPhone and iPad
  • iOS 18 or later
  • No account needed
  • Syncs with your own iCloud

Widgets everywhere

Small, medium, large, Lock Screen and StandBy. What you write is exactly what the widget shows.

Stacks that cycle

Paste your affirmations one per line and they become a Stack — a new card every hour, in order or shuffled, or on a tap.

Real notes

Rich text, checklists you can tick from the widget, photos, sketches, voice notes, and reminders with a heads-up.

Paper you'd pick

Candy papers, photo backgrounds that keep text readable, a curated font library, and stickers.

Yours, privately

No accounts, no analytics, no ads. Notes live on your device and in your own iCloud.
