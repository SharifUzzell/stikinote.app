How do I put a note on my Home Screen?

Touch and hold the Home Screen, tap Edit, then Add Widget, and search for Stiki. Pick a size, then touch and hold the widget and choose Edit Widget to select the note or Stack it shows.

My widget didn't update

Widgets refresh when you save a note and on a schedule iOS controls. Opening Stiki refreshes every widget. If one is stuck, remove it and add it again.

Are my notes backed up?

If you are signed in to iCloud, notes sync to your other devices and are included in your iCloud data. Stiki has no account of its own.

I bought Premium on another device

Open any note's sticker tool, and on the Premium screen tap Restore Purchases. Purchases follow your Apple Account.

Where did Archive go?

Archived notes are in Settings → Archived notes, where you can restore them or delete them for good.
